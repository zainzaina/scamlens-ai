About ScamLens AI

A decision-support tool for the age of AI-powered scams.

Online scams cause billions of dollars in losses worldwide every year — and most victims are not careless. They are targeted with messages designed to bypass rational thinking.

Many scams succeed because they manipulate emotions instead of exploiting technology. A fake bank alert triggers fear. A "job offer" triggers hope. An "investment opportunity" triggers greed. Fraudsters have industrialized these techniques.

ScamLens AI analyzes language patterns, manipulation techniques and suspicious indicators inside a message, screenshot or link, and returns a structured risk report with an explanation you can actually learn from.

What ScamLens does well

  • • Explains why content looks dangerous, not just that it is.
  • • Detects psychological manipulation (urgency, fear, authority, greed, scarcity).
  • • Surfaces concrete red flags (credential requests, spoofing, suspicious links).
  • • Turns each analysis into a short educational moment.

What it is not

ScamLens AI provides AI-assisted risk analysis and should be used as a decision support tool. It does not claim perfect detection. It is not a replacement for professional cybersecurity investigation, incident response, or law-enforcement reporting. When in doubt, do not click, do not reply, and contact the impersonated organization through an official channel you already trust.
