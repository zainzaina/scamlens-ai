Privacy

Browser-only. ScamLens AI has no backend and no database. Your API key, preferences and analysis history are stored in your browser's localStorage.

Third-party AI processing. When you submit content for analysis, it is sent directly from your browser to the Groq API (server-side), and is subject to Google's terms and privacy policy.

Sensitive data. Do not paste passwords, one-time codes, or full identity numbers. If a screenshot contains such information, redact it first.

Clearing data. Use "Clear all" on the History page, or clear site data in your browser to remove everything.
