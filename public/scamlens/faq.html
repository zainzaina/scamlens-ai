FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. ScamLens AI runs entirely in your browser. Your history is stored in your device's localStorage.

Do I need an API key?

No. AI analysis is powered by Groq via a secure server-side proxy — no key setup required.

Is my data sent anywhere?

The content you submit is forwarded to the Groq API through our server to run the AI analysis. Nothing is persisted on our side; your history stays in your browser's localStorage.

Can I analyze screenshots?

Yes. Upload a PNG/JPG on the Analyzer page. A vision model performs OCR and semantic analysis together.

Can it detect every scam?

No AI is perfect. Treat the results as decision support. When in doubt, verify through an official channel you already trust.

Does it work offline?

The interface installs as a PWA and shows an offline landing page, but AI analysis requires internet to reach the Groq API.
